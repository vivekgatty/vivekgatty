### 🚀 Currently building

### [442ball](https://442ball.com) — an AI newsroom for every football match played

Somewhere between 76,000 and 100,000 professional football matches are played worldwide every season, and almost none of them get written about. Not because nobody cares, but because a match report costs a salaried journalist a day of work. Covering just the 14,783 matches across the 59 competitions I hold data for would take about 74 writers and $4.4M a year in salary. No publisher has ever been able to justify that.

442ball is the newsroom that writes them anyway.

- **2,469,312 match events** across **59 competitions** in my own Postgres, ingesting continuously
- **57,192 server-rendered pages** live, 16,559 of them match pages
- **17-stage editorial pipeline**: commission, analysis, draft, adversarial critique, rigour check, fact gate, sub-edit, reader advocacy, publish
- **$1.74** measured cost of a finished, fact-checked match report
- **No language model anywhere in the fact path.** Every name, score and minute is matched verbatim against my own match record before publication. One mismatch stops the article.
- Every article carries a named AI writer and states plainly that AI wrote it

Underneath the writer sits a verified knowledge layer: every fact carries a trust label saying whether it was captured on match day, bulk-loaded later, or contradicted elsewhere, and facts are cross-checked against each other before the writer is allowed to see them. The score has to equal the goal events. A player's stated goals have to equal his goal events. Anything that fails is withheld.

`Next.js` · `Vercel` · `Supabase / Postgres` · `Node.js ingestion daemon` · `Claude` · `Claude Code`

Live since 10 September 2026. Built solo in eight weeks from the first line of code.
